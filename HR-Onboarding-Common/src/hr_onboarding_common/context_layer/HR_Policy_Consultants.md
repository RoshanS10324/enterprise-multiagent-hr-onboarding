## Discipline and Decorum

Employees shall endeavour to maintain order in the workplace in accordance with instructions received from persons designated as their superiors under the personnel system. The duties assigned to them must be carried out.

## Attendance

All staff members must report to their workplace every working day as per their chosen working hours unless they have been assigned to a client location by their supervisor. If any staff member is late for 2 days or more, appropriate action will be taken by their immediate supervisors and report the same to HR department. All supervisors are requested to monitor their subordinates’ attendance

## Dress code

Employees must always be dressed in formals at any given time. Apart from being professional, we also need to portray a professional image of our organisation. Female employees may have to wear Abaya as per the policy of the company/client.

## Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the office premises, including the toilets. Authorities will designate areas where smoking is permitted.

## Working for other than the Employer/ Concurrent external employment

It is a serious offence for any staff to work for anybody else – individual or company other than what is assigned to the employee - part time or full time. While employed with the Company, an employee may not under any circumstances hold any part-time or concurrent position in any other company, organisation, union or association. Similarly, employees cannot freelance their skills and services while in the employment of the Company. Such act will result in immediate dismissal from the Company on disciplinary grounds.

## Business hours

The normal working hours for employees – Employees onsite and dealing with clients directly, may follow client timings/holidays to ensure work is not impacted. All employees are expected to put in additional hours as and when necessary to fulfil their duties and meet target deadlines.

## Being away from the workplace

Employees must notify/obtain prior permission from their reporting heads if they need to come in late or leave early, or if they need to be away from the workplace during working hours on official or personal business.

## Working outside business hours

- Depending on the work situation, the company may require employees to work outside normal business hours and on holidays notwithstanding the provisions made in this policy.
- Employees are not eligible for any overtime payment.
- Employees will not be required to work on Saturday & Sunday unless it is due to some business priority. Prior approval to be taken from respective managers if an employee has to come on Saturday / Sunday to office for working.
- Employees will not be required to work on public holidays unless it is due to some business priority.
- If an employee is required to work on a Saturday/Sunday or public holiday, he/she will be granted leave on another day of the week (unless it is on account of delays in meeting deadlines due to personal or productivity reasons) mutually agreed upon with the concerned departmental manager and approved by the HR unless such work was due to non-achievement of the task assigned to him originally.

## Employment Contracts

All employment contracts will be limited contract. Duration of employment will be mentioned in employment contract. On completion of fixed duration, either contract will be terminated or an addendum to the existing contract stating the period of extension will be given to the employee.

All contracts are a binding agreement between the employee and the employer. This is bound by UAE Federal Law to protect the rights of the employee and the employer. Remuneration and all terms and conditions will be governed by the employment contract.

## Termination with notice

An employee may be terminated with the number of days’ notice specified in his/her offer letter, or immediately by paying salary in lieu of the notice period (less any deductions) for the following reasons:

- The employee is not able to do his job due to mental or physical illness, infirmity, weakness or senility due to age or disease.
- The employee’s skills are unsatisfactory.
- The employee’s attitude is unsatisfactory.
  <!-- Page 3 -->
- The employee does not comply with orders or instructions, or disturbs or attempts to disturb the order in the workplace.
- The employee shows no improvement despite being penalised.
- When the employee’s role becomes redundant.

## Termination without notice

An employee may be dismissed without notice for any of the reasons given below after conducting a written investigation with her/him and the dismissal decision shall be in writing and justified and the Company or its representative shall hand it over to the employee in any of the following cases:

- It is proven that the worker impersonated another person, or submitted forged certificates or documents.
- The worker committed a mistake that resulted in gross physical losses to the employer or the he deliberately damaged the properties of the employer and he acknowledged the same, provided that the latter informs the Ministry of the incident within (7) seven working days from the date of being aware of the occurrence of the incident.
- The worker violated the instructions of the establishment’s by-law related to the safety of work and workers or the workplace, provided that they are written and hung in a visible place and the worker is informed of the same.
- The worker did not perform his basic duties according to the employment contract and he continued breaching them despite conducting a written investigation with him for this reason and he is notified and warned of dismissal twice if this is repeated.
- The worker disclosed one work secret related to industrial or intellectual property, which resulted in losses to the employer, missed opportunity or achieving a personal benefit for the worker.
- The worker is drunk during working hours, is under the influence of narcotics or psychotropic substances, or commits an action breaching the public morals at the workplace.
- The worker assaults during work, the employer, manager in charge, one of his superiors or colleagues at work, by word, action or any form of assault that is punishable under the legislation in force in the State.
- The worker is absent without a legitimate reason or excuse accepted by the employer for more than (20) twenty intermittent days during one year or more than (7) seven consecutive days.
- The worker exploited his position in an illegal way to obtain personal results and gains.
  <!-- Page 4 -->
- The worker joins another establishment without abiding by the rules and procedures prescribed in this regard.

An employee terminated as per the clauses in this sub-section will not be entitled to any end of service benefits at the time of cancellation of the visa.

## Reprimands

If any of the activities below applies to an employee, he/she shall be subject to an immediate termination or reprimand, which, if ignored, will be followed by termination of employment:

- The employee frequently arrives late, leaves early or is absent without notice.
- The employee falsifies company documents or reports.
- The employee is negligent, conducts himself improperly, and frequently violates company policy.
- The employee does not obey an order from the company or from a superior.
- The employee is found misusing the computer or internet facility, telephone or any other asset of the company.
- The employee, intentionally or through negligence, causes a loss or damage to the company building, equipment, or other assets.
- The employee sullies his/her reputation as an employee of the company by committing an improper or immoral act.
- The employee is found indulging in office politics.
- The employee is found misusing the Company stationery.
- The employee is found doing personal work during office time without due permission.

## Resignation

- Employees who wish to leave the company must submit their resignation through their managers. All managers must find out the reason behind the resignation and evaluate whether a solution can be found to retain the employee. If the employee cannot be retained, then the resignation has to be accepted by his/her manager.

- The accepted resignation is then handed over to the Human Resources Department who will process it.

- The employee must give the number of days’ notice as per their offer letters or employment contract in writing and continue their normal duties until the notice period is over.
  <!-- Page 5 -->

- If an employee wishes to leave, he/she will have to serve a minimum notice period of 30 days or as specified in the employment contract, depending on the situation.

  For example, if he/she is working on a project where the project leader/manager feels that the employee is critical to the completion of the project, the employee will have to carry out the instructions given by the project leader/manager. The notice period will not exceed that specified in the employee’s offer letter. If the project leader/manager feels that the employee can be relieved before the minimum notice period is served, and if the employee wishes to leave earlier than the notice period as outlined in the offer letter, he/she will have to pay the company in lieu of the balance notice period. The earlier departure is totally at the discretion of the company.

- Final Settlement will be released and transferred into employees’ bank account after receiving the Exit form approved by his/her Reporting head.

## Loss of employee status

An employee loses employee status under the following circumstances:

- When he/she dies while in the employment of the company
- When a request for resignation is approved and the notice period is over
- When an employee is dismissed or his services are terminated by the company

## Returning company property

When an employee resigns or is dismissed, he/she must return all company property to the Administration Department. This includes office keys, Identity/Access Cards, mobile phones, laptops and any other items given to them. The appropriate form is to be filled in by the employee and submitted to the Administration Department, which will verify and hand over the form with its remarks to the Human Resources Department.

Any items lost or damaged will be deducted from the employee’s final settlement. The No-Objection Certificate will not be issued to any employee until the above procedure is completed.

## Airfare and tickets

One-way ticket is provided to an employee when he/she joins if hired outside UAE. If an employee resigns or is terminated, the Company will provide a one-way ticket to him/her to his country of origin. In case of resignation, employees should complete the contract as per their offer letter to be eligible for a one way return ticket. The one-way return ticket is applicable only if an employee is not transferring his visa to a local company.

Company will not provide annual tickets unless specified in the employment contract.

## Medical Insurance

During the employment period, company will provide medical insurance to the employees pursuant to UAE laws and not to their dependents (unless otherwise is agreed by the management or in your employment contract). All the terms and conditions of the medical coverage will be outlined when an employee joins the organization.

Management has the right to withdraw the medical insurance or change the medical insurance provider or insurance policy at their discretion any time without notice.

## Leave categories

Relevant articles of the UAE Labour Law, Dubai Internet City regulations and company policies govern the leave rules for Enterprise employees. All employees are eligible for the following categories of leave:

- Annual leave (30 calendar day for each year of service, including weekends)
- Sick leave

Employees who are on less than 6 months contract are not eligible for any kind of leave.

Any kind leave taken will need to be informed and approved by the client and also updated to the HR accordingly in advance.

## Annual leave

- All employees in UAE are eligible for 30 days (calendar day, including weekends) of paid annual leave that can be taken only after completion of one year from the date of joining.
- Employees who have completed a year and taken their first annual leave must complete 11 months of service, while the 12th month will be the leave period. If any employee wishes to avail leave before completion of one year, he/she can apply subject to approval of the reporting head and HR.
- The figure of 30 days’ annual leave is calculated on the basis of 2½ days of paid leave per month.
- The salary for paid leave will be credited to the employee’s account through the regular monthly payroll and will not be paid/credited in advance.
- All employees are required to submit their tentative leave plans to the Human Resources Department at the beginning of every calendar year after consulting their project leaders/managers. Project leaders/managers have the right to alter approved leave plans for their team members should business priorities dictate such a course of action.
  <!-- Page 7 -->
- Employees must submit the prescribed annual leave application to the Human Resources Department at least 60 days in advance.
- Encashment of annual leave is not permitted.

An employee cannot accumulate his/her leave beyond 30 days.

At the time of termination or resignation, leave salary will be given maximum only up to 30 days. Leave salary will be paid on basic and HRA when due which comprises to 72% of the total salary.

## Sick leave

- Employees are not entitled to paid sick leave during probation.
- After probation, employees will be eligible for either continuous or interrupted sick leave of up to 15 days.
- Medical certificates as per Government Health Authority rules and regulations must be produced for these sick leave periods. No sick leave will be approved without a proper medical certificate issued by a Government Health Authority.
- Calculation of sick leave deduction will be based on the gross salary.
- If an employee’s illness is directly caused by his/her misconduct – such as excessive consumption of alcohol or the use of narcotics – he/she is not entitled to any salary during the leave period.
- Absence for one day must be supported with an acceptable medical certificate as per Government Health Authority rules. Without such a certificate, the absence will be regarded as unauthorised and the leave will be deducted from his or her annual leave.
- Employees must obtain prior approval from HR through their project leaders/managers in case of any planned medical treatment.
- Sick leave calculation will be from date of joining to completion of one year and will continue in the same pattern.

> **Sick Leave and Annual Leave cannot be combined.**

## Long-term absence due to sickness

If an employee is unable to return to work beyond availing 90 days’ sick leave, the company may terminate his employment. The employee retains his/her entitlement to gratuity payments.

## Leave during probation

| Leave type | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Annual leave | Employees on probation are not entitled to annual leave. Deductions will be made from the gross salary for the calendar days an employee has been absent. |
| Sick leave | Employees on probation are not entitled to paid sick leave. Deductions will be made from the gross salary for the calendar days an employee has been absent. |

> Please note that annual leave and sick leave cannot be availed together.

## Leave framework

- An employee shall not work for another employer during any leave period or otherwise. If the employer has proof of this breach of contract, then the employee’s service will be terminated without notice and without remuneration of leave.

- If any employee fails to report back to duty on the due date and for 14 days thereafter without any intimation, it will be understood that the employee has abandoned employment with the company. He/she will be declared terminated without any remuneration.

- An employee applying for leave – whether it is 1-2 days or annual leave – must fill in the leave details in advance. The leave details, along with a list of projects, keys in the employee’s charge and important tasks or follow-ups, must be submitted to the concerned project leader/manager in writing. The project leader/manager accepts the material handed over by the employee. Both parties must maintain a copy of the handover document.

- In case illness or any other emergency prevents an employee from returning to work, he/she must call his/her immediate project leader/manager and make arrangements to hand over keys, tasks and anything else that is critical to the functioning of the organisation. The project leader/manager should inform the Human Resources Department accordingly. Necessary documented evidence must be provided to the company under such emergency situations.

- A nominated person temporarily takes over the responsibilities from the absent employee for the smooth functioning of the company. Charge of these duties is returned to the employee when he/she resumes duty.

## Remunerations and Reimbursements

### Payroll

The monthly salary, as specified in the employment contract, will be transferred into employees’ bank account on the specified date after receiving the approved timesheets with appropriate deductions due from the employee. Salary will be transferred to the bank account and no cash will be given unless and until employment visa/Emirates ID is under process

### Account details

When an employee joins the company, a bank account has to be opened by himself/herself. The Human Resources Department will issue the required letter from the company to him/her. After opening the account, the employee must submit his/her account number to the Human Resources/Finance department.

If an employee wishes to change the bank account for salary transfer, he/she must submit No Liability certificate from the existing bank to HR and Finance Department.

### End of service benefits/gratuity

The term “basic” denotes the basic wage specified in the contract of employment. No allowances of any kind are included in the basic component. For purposes of gratuity calculation, the last drawn basic salary will be taken into account. Allowances are not taken into consideration while calculating gratuity.

If an employee who completes one year or more years of continuous service, he/she is entitled to end-of-service benefits/gratuity calculated as follows:

a) 21 days’ salary for each year during the first five years of service

b) 30 days’ salary for each year following the initial five-year service period

An employee who has not completed one year of continuous service is not entitled to any gratuity if he/she has resigned or has been terminated.

The maximum gratuity payment an employee is eligible for is two years’ salary. Any amount due from the employee to the employer will be deducted from the end-of-service benefits payable.

### Leave Salary

Other end-of-service benefits include leave salary. At the time of termination or resignation, leave salary will be given maximum only up to 30 days. Leave salary will be paid on basic and HRA (72%) when due.
