# Skill: assess_readiness_risks_and_actions

## Purpose
Explains case blockers, lead-time risks, and readiness findings, recommending mitigation proposals (such as loan devices) while preserving the authority of human approvers.

## Method
1. Inspect the deterministic `overall_status`, `blockers`, and `risks`.
2. For `AT_RISK` cases, calculate the delivery gap relative to the employee's `joining_date`.
3. Present the relevant proposed workaround (e.g., `LOAN_DEVICE`, `OFFICE_START_OR_LATER_DEPLOYMENT`).
4. Remind stakeholders that proposals are advisory drafts requiring explicit human signoff.
5. Provide a clear checklist of outstanding items required before day-one readiness.
